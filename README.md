Sakhi (सखी) is a Sanskrit word meaning female friend, companion, or confidante. That meaning defines our vision: to create an AI-powered wellness companion that stands beside women, helping them move from information to understanding, understanding to confidence, and confidence to action.

**The Problem** Health information is everywhere, but information alone does not make healthcare accessible. For women in underserved communities, language barriers, limited health literacy, affordability, fragmented access to care, cultural barriers, and unreliable connectivity can make even basic wellness decisions difficult. The real gap is not simply access to information. It is the gap between having information and knowing what it means, what to ask, where to go, and what to do next.

**Our Solution** Sakhi is an AI-powered, women-first global wellness companion designed to close that gap. Through one simple and inclusive experience, Sakhi helps women understand wellness information, ask questions conversationally, explore affordable care options, prepare for healthcare conversations, receive personalized wellness and dietary guidance, and retain useful information for low-connectivity situations. Instead of expecting women to adapt to complex technology, Sakhi adapts the experience to the woman, including her language, region, accessibility needs, and connectivity context.

**Key Features**

💬 **Chat with Sakhi** A conversational AI experience where women can ask wellness questions naturally and receive information in clear, approachable language.

🌍 **Global & Multilingual Experience** Users can select their country or region and preferred language, creating the foundation for a more localized and culturally aware experience.

🏥 **Affordable Care Discovery** Sakhi can help users explore relevant and affordable care options when additional professional support may be needed.

🌿 **Learn & Understand** Complex wellness information can be translated into simpler, easier-to-understand explanations, helping users become more informed participants in their wellness journey.

🥗 **Personalized Wellness Guidance** Sakhi can support personalized dietary and everyday wellness guidance based on the user's context and goals.

💬 P**repare for Care Conversations** Sakhi can help women better understand a concern and prepare questions before speaking with a healthcare professional, helping them approach those conversations with greater confidence.

📶 L**ow-Connectivity & Offline-Friendly Experience** Important wellness guidance can be designed for lightweight or offline access, extending the value of Sakhi to communities where reliable internet connectivity cannot be assumed.

**Technology Vision**

Sakhi is envisioned as an AI-first, multilingual and globally adaptable wellness experience built with Microsoft technologies.

The solution can bring together capabilities such as:

- Azure OpenAI for conversational and contextual AI experiences
- Microsoft Copilot / Copilot Studio for intelligent conversational experiences and orchestration
- Microsoft Foundry as part of the AI development and experimentation ecosystem
- Speech capabilities to help make the experience more accessible through voice interaction
- Multilingual experiences to reduce language barriers
- Responsible AI principles to support safe, transparent, privacy-conscious experiences
- Mobile-first and low-connectivity design so the experience can extend beyond users with constant high-speed internet access
- The goal is not to use AI simply because it is available. The goal is to use AI where it can make an otherwise complicated journey simpler, more understandable, more inclusive, and more human.

**Our Vision**

We envision a future where a woman's location, language, digital literacy, affordability, or connectivity does not determine how confidently she can understand and navigate her wellness journey. Sakhi is not designed to replace healthcare professionals. It is designed to help women become better informed, better prepared, and more confident participants in their own wellness journey.

**Sakhi is not just AI that answers. It is AI that accompanies.**
